# Viral TTS cases: what spread when the core was TTS alone or LLM + TTS

Research date: 2026-10-15. Scope: global and India, 2023–2026, weighted to 2025–2026.
Class key: **TTS** = text in, voice out. **LLM+TTS** = generated script, then voice. **STT** = needs speech input.
Numbers marked "(unverified)" could not be confirmed from a primary source.

## 1. Cases (appended as found)

### 1.1 TikTok's built-in TTS voice ("Jessie", and the earlier Bev Standing voice) — TTS
- **What:** TikTok's text-to-speech button reads on-screen captions in a stock voice. The first voice (Bev Standing) was replaced in 2021 by "Jessie", voiced by ex-radio host Kat Callaghan; by 2022 it was "ubiquitous" ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/9888850/what-happened-to-tiktok-text-to-speech-voice/amp), [GMA](https://goodmorningamerica.com/gma/story/meet-woman-tiktoks-famous-jessie-voice-92990300)).
- **Numbers:** Callaghan's Oct 2022 reveal video passed 43M views ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/9888850/what-happened-to-tiktok-text-to-speech-voice/amp)).
- **Why it spread:** zero-effort narration for people who don't want to use their own voice, and the irony of a peppy synthetic voice over grim or serious footage ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/9888850/what-happened-to-tiktok-text-to-speech-voice/amp)). The voice became a recognisable *character*, not just a utility.
- **Share artefact:** the video itself; the voice is baked into native TikTok posts. Shared by ordinary creators.

### 1.2 NotebookLM Audio Overviews (Sept 2024 →) — LLM+TTS
- **What:** upload documents, get a two-host "podcast" discussing them. Launched Sept 2024 and went viral in Oct 2024 ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/10/04/google-ai-podcast-notebooklm), [MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2024/10/03/1104978/people-are-using-google-study-software-to-make-ai-podcasts-and-theyre-weird-and-amazing/amp/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NotebookLM)).
- **Numbers:** "more than four million monthly users" per web data, undated (unverified) ([Express & Star/PA](https://www.expressandstar.com/uk-news/notebooklm-how-googles-ai-powered-note-taking-app-took-off-by-adding-audio-5753408)); Discord of ~65k members (unverified, secondary summary) ([summify](https://summify.io/discover/how-notebooklm-was-made-v1mOId/)).
- **Why it spread:** the *format* was the hook — two hosts bantering, interrupting, saying "right?!" — not raw voice quality. People fed it absurd inputs (a CV, a single word "poop", the hosts "discovering they are AI") and posted the results ([MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2024/10/03/1104978/people-are-using-google-study-software-to-make-ai-podcasts-and-theyre-weird-and-amazing/amp/)).
- **Share artefact:** screen-recorded clips and audio snippets on X/Reddit/TikTok. Shared by tech early adopters first, then students.
- **No speech input at all.** Pure text → script → two voices.

### 1.3 Spotify Wrapped AI podcast (Dec 2024) — LLM+TTS on personal data
- **What:** a personalised two-host audio overview of your year in music, built with NotebookLM tech; English, 7 markets, limited time, shareable/downloadable ([Spotify newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/?p=32183), [Google blog](https://blog.google/technology/google-labs/notebooklm-spotify-wrapped/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/04/spotify-wrapped-2024-adds-an-ai-podcast-powered-by-googles-notebooklm)).
- **Numbers (Wrapped overall, not the podcast alone):** 2024 Wrapped took ~62 h to reach 200M engaged users and reached 245M total; 2025 hit 200M in ~24 h with ~500M shares (+41% YoY), company-reported ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-wrapped-campaign-hit-200m-engaged-users-in-24-hours-a-19-yoy-increase)). No separate share count for the AI podcast was found (unverified).
- **Why it spread:** Wrapped's engine is "about me" data plus a calendar moment. The audio was an add-on; the cards (images) remain the main shared artefact.
- **Lesson:** personalised audio rides on an existing self-expression ritual; a visual card is still what people post.

### 1.4 "AI presidents gaming" (Feb 2023 →) — TTS with cloned voices
- **What:** Biden, Trump, Obama etc. "playing" video games and bickering, using ElevenLabs-style cloned voices over still images with headsets ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/ai-presidents-gaming), [Inverse](https://inverse.com/gaming/youtube-ai-presidential-gaming-debates)).
- **Why it spread:** famous voices in a banal, low-status situation (incongruity), with a scripted comic dialogue. Exploded when ElevenLabs became public in early 2023 ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/ai-voice-game-modding-game-characters)).
- **Share artefact:** YouTube/TikTok videos (static image + voice track). Script written by humans or LLMs; **no STT**. Depends on cloning, which Bulbul Flash lacks — but the *structure* (known character archetypes arguing about trivia) transfers to stock voices with strong personas.

### 1.5 Italian brainrot (Jan 2025 →) — TTS (+ AI images/video)
- **What:** absurd AI animal-object hybrids (Tralalero Tralala, Bombardiro Crocodilo, Ballerina Cappuccina) narrated by a male Italian **text-to-speech** voice rhyming the character name with nonsense. First upload early Jan 2025 by @eZburger401 on TikTok ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/italian-brainrot-ai-italian-animals), [KYM guide](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-does-the-tralalero-tralala-meme-mean-the-origins-of-the-italian-brainrot-tiktok-trend-featuring-a-shark-wearing-nikes-explained)).
- **Numbers:** "billions of cumulative views" per a marketing how-to (unverified) ([Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/italian-brainrot-videos)). Spawned versions in other languages ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/italian-brainrot-ai-italian-animals)).
- **Why it spread:** a *named character* + a chant-like TTS line that is easy to repeat and remix; the sound itself became a reusable TikTok audio. The robotic delivery is part of the joke.
- **Share artefact:** short videos; the *sound* travels separately and gets reused in thousands of edits. Shared by kids/teens; then brands. **No STT.**
- **India note:** Indian versions exist (unverified count); the format is language-agnostic, which makes a Hindi/Tamil "desi brainrot" character pack with Bulbul plausible.

### 1.6 PhonePe SmartSpeaker in Amitabh Bachchan's voice (Sept 2023) — TTS reading money
- **What:** the UPI soundbox that announces "₹X received" switched to Amitabh Bachchan's voice, Hindi and English; merchants opt in from the PhonePe for Business app ([PhonePe press release](https://www.phonepe.com/press/phonepe-smartspeakers-introduce-a-first-of-its-kind-celebrity-voice-feature-with-shri-amitabh-bachchan/), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/quantum-brief/phonepe-collaborates-with-amitabh-bachchan-introduces-celebrity-voice-feature-13056.htm)).
- **Numbers:** 4M devices across 19,000 pin codes, 100 crore transactions announced in the first year (company-reported) ([DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/lifestyle/technology/phonepe-launches-celebrity-voice-feature-with-amitabh-on-its-smartspeakers-734437)).
- **Why it matters for us:** the most-heard TTS sentence in India is a **number read aloud in a shop**. A voice saying an amount correctly is already a daily ritual; making it funny or personal (your shop, your name, a famous-style persona) taps a format every shopkeeper knows. Agency 82.5 Communications built a campaign around it ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/advertising-news/825-communications-brings-alive-phonepe-alerts-with-amitabh-bachchans-iconic-voice-129915.html)).
- **Share artefact:** ad films and merchant videos of the speaker going off. **TTS only, no STT.**

### 1.7 AI news anchors in India: Sana (Aaj Tak, Mar 2023), Lisa (OTV, Jul 2023) — TTS (+ avatar)
- **What:** synthetic presenters reading bulletins; Sana did a full bulletin in French during PM Modi's France visit; Lisa reads in English and Odia ([SheThePeople](https://www.shethepeople.tv/news/ai-generated-news-anchor-lisa-odisha), [Techwire Asia](https://techwireasia.com/2023/07/ai-news-anchors-and-which-countries-has-them/), [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/amp/story/technology/indian-television-sees-spurt-of-ai-driven-robotic-news-anchors-in-multiple-language-channels)).
- **Why it spread:** novelty + regional language pride (Odia got CNN and SCMP coverage). Spread through news coverage more than user sharing.
- **Lesson:** "news anchor voice" is a familiar Indian register. A **parody news bulletin about your friend / your gali** reuses it — users share the joke, not the tech.

### 1.8 Cadbury #NotJustACadburyAd "Shah Rukh Khan-My-Ad" (Diwali 2021) — LLM-free template + synthetic voice/face
- **What:** a shop owner enters store name, category and location on a microsite and gets a personalised ad with Shah Rukh Khan's synthetic face and voice naming *their* shop; Rephrase.ai made the avatars ([WPP case study](https://www.wpp.com/en/case-studies/ogilvy-and-wavemaker-notjustacadburyad), [Outlook](https://outlookindia.com/national/india-news-how-the-notjustacadburyad-campaign-us-using-ai-to-bring-joy-to-local-c-news-398614), [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/shah-rukh-khan-cadbury-campaign-how-to-create-free-ads-to-support-your-local-stores-116514.html)). The 2020 edition was geo-tagged ads in 300+ pin codes ([Wavemaker](https://wavemakerglobal.com/our-work/growth-stories/shah-rukh-khan-my-ad)).
- **Numbers:** WPP lists the campaign as a case study; specific store-count and reach figures were not confirmed in this pass (unverified). It is widely cited as India's best-known personalised synthetic-voice campaign.
- **Why it spread:** *your shop's name in a star's mouth*, at Diwali, free, and the owner has a selfish reason to forward it to every customer on WhatsApp. This is almost exactly the "shopkeeper posts an AI ad on his WhatsApp status" bar.
- **Share artefact:** a short video file per store, forwarded on WhatsApp and posted as status. **No STT**; input was a form (but a tiny one: 3 fields, owner-motivated).
- **Bulbul angle:** no celebrity voice, but Flash can do the part SRK-My-Ad needed most — store name, offer price and date said correctly in the local language.

### 1.9 Reddit-story faceless videos (2021 → still running) — TTS (+ LLM rewriting since 2023)
- **What:** a Reddit post (AITA, revenge stories) read by a TTS voice over Minecraft parkour or Subway Surfers footage. Pipeline: copy post → script → AI voiceover → background gameplay ([Fliki](https://fliki.ai/blog/best-faceless-youtube-niches), [easyviral](https://easyviral.ai/blog/how-much-do-faceless-youtube-channels-make-2026)).
- **Numbers:** RPM claims of $1–5 from vendor blogs (unverified, conflicting) ([easyviral](https://easyviral.ai/blog/how-much-do-faceless-youtube-channels-make-2026)). Described as the most saturated faceless niche.
- **Why it spread:** drama + split attention (story audio, hypnotic gameplay video). TTS is just a cheap narrator; the *content* is the drama.
- **Lesson:** a supply-side format (made for creators to farm views), not a share-to-friends format. Low fit for a judged demo.

### 1.10 Gemini Storybook (2025) — LLM+TTS (+ images)
- **What:** prompt → 10-page illustrated book with read-aloud narration, 45+ languages, can use your own photos/kids' drawings ([gemini.google](https://gemini.google/overview/storybook/), [Geekflare](https://geekflare.com/news/gemini-can-now-turn-your-ideas-into-illustrated-storybooks)).
- **Viral?** No evidence of a breakout share moment was found (unverified). Bedtime-story products are *used* a lot (private, at night) but rarely *posted*. Personal, low-arousal, no audience.
- **Lesson:** bedtime stories are a retention play, not a viral one, unless the child's name + a funny twist makes a parent post it.

### 1.11 Veo 3 "Prompt Theory" and AI vox-pops (May–June 2025) — LLM/video model with native speech
- **What:** Veo 3 generates 8-second clips *with* spoken dialogue; Hashem Al-Ghaili's "Prompt Theory" montage showed people insisting they are not AI prompts; it spawned a genre of fake street interviews ([Chortle](https://www.chortle.co.uk/punching-ups/2025/05/27/58166), [Hybrid Horizons](https://hybridhorizons.substack.com/p/the-prompt-theory-phenomenon)).
- **Numbers:** no reliable view count found (unverified). Creator newsletters cite 10M–28M views for other Veo 3 genres (unverified) ([rpn](https://rpn.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-go-viral-with-veo-3)).
- **Why it spread:** speech is what made AI video feel real; the *talking* character carried the joke. **No STT**; text prompt in, speech out.
- **Lesson:** in 2025–26 people expect AI video to talk. A plain voice track over a still card now competes with talking video. Bulbul's edge must be *Indian language and numbers done right*, not realism.

### 1.12 "Harry Potter by Balenciaga" (Mar 2023) — TTS with cloned voices + image stills
- **What:** Midjourney stills + cloned actor voices (ElevenLabs cited) + techno; spawned "X by Balenciaga" and "Harry Potter but in X" waves ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/balenciaga-ai-videos), [Man of Many](https://www.manofmany.com/entertainment/viral-ai-harry-potter-balenciaga)).
- **Numbers:** 3.6M–11M YouTube views depending on source/date (conflicting) ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/balenciaga-ai-videos), [Man of Many](https://www.manofmany.com/entertainment/viral-ai-harry-potter-balenciaga)).
- **Why it spread:** a **template** ("famous IP × unexpected style") that anyone could copy with a new pair. Very few voice lines; the voice gave each still a character.
- **Lesson:** a repeatable *template with a slot* ("X but as Y") spreads further than one great clip.

### 1.13 Duolingo's Lily and character voices — TTS (persona)
- **What:** Duolingo's deadpan goth character (2020 redesign) became a meme ("I'm such a Lily"); "Living With Lily" shorts got ~10.5M views and 100k followers (company-reported) ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/lily-duolingo-goth-girl), [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/391235), [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91225538/duolingo-ambitious-plan-viral-owl-mascot)). In-app character lines are TTS; Video Call with Lily later added STT.
- **Why it spread:** a **consistent persona** with a recognisable delivery (flat, bored) that fans could identify with. Personality beats voice quality.
- **Lesson:** a named character with one strong attitude (strict Sharma uncle, drama aunty, sarcastic Mumbai auto-wala) is more shareable than a neutral voice.

### 1.14 NotebookLM in Hindi and 14+ Indian languages (Apr 2025) — LLM+TTS
- **What:** Audio Overviews expanded to 50+ languages on 29 Apr 2025 incl. Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu; now 80+ ([Google blog](https://blog.google/technology/google-labs/notebooklm-audio-overviews-50-languages), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/04/29/notebooklm-audio-overviews-languages/), [Google support](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16212820)).

### 1.15 Instagram AI dubbing of Reels into Indian languages (Hindi first; +Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi announced Nov 2025) — **needs STT**
- **What:** Meta AI dubs a creator's reel into another language in the creator's own voice, optional lip-sync ([afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/instagram-adds-ai-voice-translations-in-five-indian-languages-10820930), [Madhyamam](https://madhyamamonline.com/amp/technology/instagram-expands-ai-dubbing-and-lip-sync-for-reels-to-five-indian-languages-1486671)).

### 1.16 "Yaatri kripya dhyan dein" railway-announcement parody reel (late 2024 / 2025) — announcement register
- **What:** an animated Instagram reel parodying Indian Railways delay announcements went viral; commenters asked for it as a ringtone and joked about "childhood trauma" of cancelled trains ([My Pune Pulse](https://www.mypunepulse.com/?p=120072)). Whether its voice was synthetic is not stated (unverified).
- **Why it spread:** **nostalgia + a register every Indian can hear in their head** (the three-tone chime, "gaadi sankhya…", the number read digit-by-digit). The ringtone requests show people want the *audio* itself.
- **Bulbul angle:** railway announcements are mostly numbers, times and place names — exactly what Flash reads well. A "your friend's life as a railway announcement" generator has a native register and a ringtone/status artefact. No direct evidence of a personalised AI version going viral was found (gap).

### 1.17 Personalised festive greeting videos from a star, by name (India, Diwali 2024) — template TTS/cloned voice, sent on WhatsApp
- **Royal Stag × Gan.AI:** Suryakumar Yadav greeting each fan by name; 100,000+ unique video copies, delivered via WhatsApp (vendor-reported) ([Gan.AI case study](https://www.gan.studio/blog/case-studies/royal-stag)).
- **itel × Gan.AI:** Hrithik Roshan greeting dealers by name, seven festival versions incl. Diwali, sent via WhatsApp ([Gan.AI](https://www.gan.studio/blog/case-studies/itel)).
- **Behrouz Biryani "Nawabi Surprise":** QR on the box unlocks a Saif Ali Khan AI greeting; redirected to WhatsApp for forwarding; users could make several for loved ones ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/advertising-news/saif-ali-khan-delivers-diwali-message-for-behrouz-biryani-138219.html)).
- **Counter-trend:** Coca-Cola's "Say no to forwarded greetings" Diwali campaign — brands fighting generic forwards ([afaqs](https://afaqs.com/news/marketing-initiatives/say-no-to-forwarded-festive-greetings-with-coca-colas-diwaili-campaign)).
- **Numbers:** view/share counts not published (unverified). The repeated choice of *WhatsApp* as the channel across brands is itself the signal.
- **Why it spreads:** your name + festival + a voice you didn't expect, landing in the channel where Indians already forward greetings. **No STT**; one name field.
- **Bulbul angle:** Diwali is 8 Nov 2026, Dhanteras 6 Nov — the challenge ends right as greeting season starts. Flash says names right in 11 languages; the missing piece (star voice) can be replaced by a strong persona.

### 1.18 ElevenLabs v3 audio tags (June 2025, GA later) — TTS
- **What:** inline tags like `[laughs]`, `[whispers]`, `[sighs]` direct emotional delivery ([ElevenLabs blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v3), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/elevenlabs-eleven-v3-lets-ai-voices-whisper-laugh-and-express-emotions-naturally/)).
- **Viral?** spread among creators as demos; no share numbers found (unverified).
- **Lesson:** the global bar for "expressive" TTS now includes laughs and whispers. Bulbul V4 Flash lacks them, so an entry should **not** compete on emotion; compete on Indian names, numbers, code-mixing and speed.

### 1.20 Modi AI-voice song covers and political voice notes (India, 2023 →) — cloned-voice TTS/SVC, no STT

### 1.22 "How Bad Is Your Spotify" (The Pudding, Dec 2020) — LLM-ish roast, **text only**

### 1.23 "@chatgpt_says" — ChatGPT voice glitching on absurd text (May 2025) — TTS breakdown as comedy
- **What:** a TikTok account fed ChatGPT voice absurd strings (e.g. a long run of the letter B) and posted the stuttering result; first video ~140k views, top post (24 May 2025) 3.5M views ([Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/chatgpt-says-tiktok-trend-glitch-ai-gibberish/)).
- **Class:** the viral part is TTS output of user-typed text; the voice mode itself takes speech, but the content is text → voice.
- **Why it spread:** watching a confident AI voice fail is funny; low effort, serial format.
- **Lesson / risk:** the inverse is Bulbul's pitch — *Indian tongue-twisters, long numbers, Hinglish names read perfectly*. A "can your AI say this?" challenge is a shareable demo format.

### 1.24 "NotebookLM podcast about me" (LinkedIn/CV) (Oct 2024 →) — LLM+TTS on personal data

- **Lesson:** "AI hypes me up" and "AI roasts me" are the two poles of about-me audio; both are LLM+TTS with no speech input.

### 1.28 AI cricket commentary in Hinglish / Indian languages (2024–2025) — TTS (some cloned)

## 3. Share mechanics

1. **The artefact is almost always a video, not an audio file.** Even audio-first products spread as screen recordings (NotebookLM), still-image + voice (presidents gaming, Balenciaga), or 9:16 clips (brainrot). Spotify's audio podcast rode on image cards. In India, WhatsApp forwards and Status take MP4s natively (see 1.8, 1.17).
2. **Two kinds of sharer.** (a) *Creators/meme pages* farming views (brainrot, Reddit stories, Modi covers). (b) *Ordinary people who share because the output is about them or helps them* — shop owners (Cadbury, Shikhar), fans named in a greeting (Royal Stag), professionals hyped by NotebookLM. Group (b) is who a 16-hour tool can reach.
3. **India's channel is WhatsApp**: every Indian personalised-voice campaign found (Cadbury, Royal Stag, itel, Behrouz, political voice notes) delivered on WhatsApp.
4. **The *sound* can travel without the tool**: brainrot sounds and the railway reel ("make it a ringtone") show a short audio hook gets reused. A downloadable MP4 *and* a short audio line both help.

## 4. Patterns: what TTS-only formats go viral, and why

| Pattern | Evidence | Why it spreads |
|---|---|---|
| **Your name / your shop in a familiar voice** | Cadbury, Royal Stag, itel, Shikhar, PhonePe, NotebookLM-about-me | self-presentation; the recipient has a selfish reason to forward |
| **A familiar public register, hijacked** (announcement, news, commentary, soundbox) | railway reel, Sana/Lisa, cricket, PhonePe | instant recognition, nostalgia; the register *is* the joke |
| **A named character with one attitude** | Jessie, Lily, brainrot characters, presidents | persona > voice quality; fans identify and remix |
| **A template with a slot** ("X but as Y", "Z in N languages") | Balenciaga, brainrot regional versions, Modi covers in 5 languages | anyone can make the next one; serial content |
| **Roast or hype about me** | How Bad Is Your Spotify, NotebookLM-about-me | high-arousal (amusement/flattery) + about-me |
| **Festival/calendar timing** | Wrapped (Dec), Cadbury & greetings (Diwali), saree trend (Navratri) | a reason to post *now*, repeated yearly |
| **AI failing or succeeding at something hard** | @chatgpt_says, Sana in French | spectacle; "can it do this?" challenge |

What did **not** spread by sharing: bedtime stories (private), faceless Reddit/horror channels (view farming, not friend-to-friend), generic explainer podcasts once free everywhere.

What Bulbul V4 Flash cannot do that many of these used: voice cloning (presidents, Balenciaga, Modi, star greetings), singing (covers), laughs/whispers (ElevenLabs v3). The gap is filled by **strong stock personas + Indian registers + numbers/names said right**, which none of the global tools do well in 11 Indian languages.
