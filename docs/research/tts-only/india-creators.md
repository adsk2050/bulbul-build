## 4. Gaps Bulbul can fix

- General complaint: most TTS was built English-first with Hindi added later, giving robotic stress, schwa errors (Ramesh, Ganesh) and failure on code-switching. Smallest.ai comparison blog (vendor source). https://smallest.ai/blog/english-to-hindi-text-to-speech-how-to-choose-a-bilingual-tts-tool
- No creator-forum complaints about prices or numbers found. The number/price gap is a hypothesis. Sarvam's docs say numbers, dates and currency are handled by `enable_preprocessing`, Indian digit grouping (5,00,000) is read correctly, and `language_code` sets how entities are spoken in Hinglish. https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/api-guides-tutorials/text-to-speech/best-practices
- Same docs: `_enhi_` personas for true Hinglish, native script for each language, a pronunciation dictionary for brand names, no SSML, 2,500 chars per request, ml-IN and od-IN have no v4 personas yet.
- Poster apps: no voice listed (Brands.live). Voice is the missing layer.
- Free voice tools are described as 40+ languages with Hindi but watermarks; paid Rs1,000-3,000/month. https://www.promptspace.in/blog/ai-voiceover-for-youtube-videos-free-2026

## Top 6 opportunities (TTS-only or LLM+TTS, no speech input)

Ranking is my reasoning from the gaps above, not measured demand. Dates: Karwa Chauth 29 Oct, Dhanteras 6 Nov, Diwali 8 Nov, Chhath 13-16 Nov 2026.

1. **Dukaan ka offer voice ad (shop-owner status ad).**
   - Who: kirana, jeweller, sweet shop and garment shop owners who already post daily ads.
   - Input: shop name, 2-3 offers with prices ("Rs 1,299 se shuru, 40% off"), language, tone.
   - Output: 15-30 s Hinglish/regional voiceover (LLM writes the script, Bulbul reads it) on a 9:16 price-card video, ready for WhatsApp status.
   - Why share: it is their own ad, posted daily; poster apps give silent templates.
   - Bulbul feature: Hinglish `_enhi_` ads persona, price and number reading with preprocessing, Indian digit grouping.

2. **Dhanteras / Diwali personalised voice greeting.**
   - Who: anyone sending wishes; shops greeting regular customers.
   - Input: recipient name(s), relationship, language, festival.
   - Output: 10-20 s voice card with the name spoken correctly, shareable as status or in a chat.
   - Why share: it carries their name and replaces the usual static image; festival days are peak volume (8B WhatsApp messages in India on Diwali 2015).
   - Bulbul feature: Indian name pronunciation, 11 languages, speed.

3. **Daily rate announcer (gold, vegetables, mandi, petrol).**
   - Who: jewellers, sabzi sellers and small traders who post today's rates every morning.
   - Input: table of items and prices (typed or pasted).
   - Output: daily "aaj ka bhav" voice + card reel.
   - Why share: a recurring daily post; saves typing and speaking; customers forward it.
   - Bulbul feature: reading many numbers, decimals and currency correctly.

4. **Faceless explainer / finance-tip Shorts in Hindi and regional languages.**
   - Who: faceless-channel creators (finance, education, food hacks).
   - Input: topic or link; LLM writes the script in the chosen language.
   - Output: narrated audio per short, ready for captions and stock footage.
   - Why share: daily volume; AI narrators are the standard method in Indian guides.
   - Bulbul feature: natural Hindi prosody, multiple languages (cheaper than the Rs1,000-3,000/month tools, per an unverified blog).

5. **Aarti / katha narration and "Subah ka vichar" good-morning voice notes.**
   - Who: devotional and Good Morning content channels and family groups.
   - Input: deity, festival or theme; language.
   - Output: a spoken suvichar, katha or shloka over a festival image.
   - Why share: Good Morning and devotional forwards are a WhatsApp staple; this adds a voice. Demand for AI devotional voice is unconfirmed; vendors and freelancers sell it.
   - Bulbul feature: Devanagari and regional script reading, pace and pitch control for a calm tone.

6. **One message, many languages (event, wedding, shop opening, society notice).**
   - Who: small organisers, RWA admins, wedding hosts, new shop owners.
   - Input: one line in any language plus date, time and address.
   - Output: the same announcement voiced in 2-3 languages.
   - Why share: invites go to mixed-language groups; one voice note replaces typing.
   - Bulbul feature: 11 languages, date/time/number reading, translation + TTS.

Side note: Sarvam's docs list ml-IN and od-IN as having no v4 personas yet, so avoid Malayalam and Odia in a v4-Flash demo.
